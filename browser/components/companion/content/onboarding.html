What's new on flowstate

June 21, 2022

Quickly search your history
Trying to find a document, article, or page you’ve been to before? Just go to Browse > History > Search my history, and type a keyword to search your Flowstate history.
Your full day of meetings, at a glance
Get your rundown of the day’s upcoming meetings. Go to Browse > Calendar and ta-da! You’ll see the entire day’s calendar events.
Windows users: Scale up your text
Flowstate now responds to Windows text scaling. Go to Settings > Ease of Access > Vision > Display > Make your text bigger.
Do more from event cards
Event cards just keep getting better and better. Expand a card to reveal more actions, like copying the meeting invite link. If you dismiss an event card, you can still go to Browse > Calendar to see upcoming meetings.
