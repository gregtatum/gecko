What's new on flowstate

July 25, 2022

Now with sound
Calming audio feedback will now play whenever you open Flowstate or set aside a session. If silence helps you flow better, sounds can be disabled in preferences.
Updates to settings
We’ve changed the settings page to be more relevant to Flowstate.
More integrations coming soon to Flowstate
We’ve updated parts of Flowstate to pave the way for new third-party integrations. You may need to reconnect your Microsoft or Google account from settings as part of this update.
Coming soon: private browsing
This feature lets you know that private browsing will arrive in a future update.
