What's new on flowstate

May 9, 2022

Retrieve your pins when you restore a session
Pins now persist on a per-session basis. When you restore a session, everything you previously pinned during that session comes back.
Get to your next meeting more quickly
The join meeting button is more visible, accessibility-friendly, and no longer blocks the event time.
Keep your snapshots fresh and tidy
To make your snapshots more relevant, Flowstate removes those you haven’t interacted with in several weeks. We’ve also cleaned up the data to improve our snapshot recommendations.
