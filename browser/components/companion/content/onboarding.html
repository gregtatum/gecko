What's new on flowstate

July 18, 2022

Snapshots just got smarter
We’re changing how snapshots appear in the companion. You’ll start seeing page suggestions based on how you use Flowstate, instead of your browsing history.
String changes
We audited and updated strings inherited from Firefox to give Flowstate a voice of its own.
Accessibility Updates
We’ve fixed several bugs affecting our Screen Reader and High Contrast Mode.
Keep your pins separated
When you pin the same views, Flowstate will now keep those views independent, instead of combining them.
See relevant quick actions
You’ll now see more relevant quick actions in the search bar based on the services you have connected.
